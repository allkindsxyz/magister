magister.cards

A 54-card deck where suit and rank hide in the painting.

Magister is a standard playing deck — four suits, thirteen ranks, two jokers — built from fifty-four original works by Dr. Klein. There are no pips and no corner indexes. You read each card from its scene. The booklet in the box is the key.

Conceived by Alexander Zhilchik. Painted by Dr. Klein (Vasily Pochitsky, 1967–2024). Every card is one of the fifty-four original works.
